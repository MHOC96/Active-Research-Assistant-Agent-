How it works

  1. Enter a research question or paste text you want to cite.
  2. The system searches your local index (ChromaDB + keyword search).
  3. If evidence is weak, it searches arXiv, OpenAlex, Semantic Scholar, and the web.
  4. arXiv PDFs are ingested into your local index; web and index hits become formatted citations.
  5. Corporate or documentation pages cite as author-date, e.g. (ServiceNow, 2023).
  6. Click highlighted sentences in your text to see the citations found for that segment.
  7. Recent queries are saved locally - click one to instantly reload its result without re-running the pipeline.
  8. Select history items and use Remove selected to delete saved results you no longer need.

Tip: Keep Fast mode on for quicker first results on new topics.
